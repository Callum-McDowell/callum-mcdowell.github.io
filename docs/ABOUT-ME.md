Hi there! I'm Callum, and I've been working as a software engineer in the field of product development for close to four years.

In that time I've worked on a multi-million dollar project throughout its full life-cycle, from concept to delivery and in-market support, and taken on a wide range of responsibilities, including: user interface (UI) development, tooling and infrastructure, testing and verification, AI and agentic workflow integration, manufacturing, compliance, and warranty (customer) support.

I've implemented user interfaces (UIs) on different platforms: embedded systems (LVGL), Android (Flutter), and desktop and mobile (Qt). I'm used to working closely with designers and facilitating collaboration between the software and user experience (UX) teams--I believe that supportive and honest relationships are what make teams effective. Along the way I've picked up experience with design systems and UI best practices (Figma, tokens, AI spec-driven code generation, etc).

Clear communication is important to me. I'm used to communicating thoroughly both internally and externally, and I have:

- worked with third-party contractors, providing and clarifying spec,
- fixed customer issues (always satisfying!),
- provided factory support and used change management tools to make releases to factory and change manufacturing processes,
- worked on compliance processes (complying with IEC standards for domestic and international markets), which involved seeking accreditation from third-party test laboratories,
- and more besides.

Tooling and infrastructure are entering a renaissance due to AI and agentic workflows, but I can say I was there first 🙂. Things I've worked on include:

- logging infrastructure,
- wirelessly collecting telemetry from test machines,
- data analysis and internal tools,
- tests and test frameworks (for unit, integration, and end-to-end tests),

Of course, in this modern age I have experience working with AI agents and investigating how to use AI to super-charge development, verification, and everything in-between. I think AI really highlights the importance of tight iterative feedback loops with stakeholders and having clear, high-quality specification and verification.

I'm used to working in an agile environment and ran a stint as a scrum master. I'm a big fan of documentation and meeting notes, and am used to all the usual tools like Jira, Confluence, Microsoft SharePoint, and the rest of the Microsoft and Google Docs suite.
